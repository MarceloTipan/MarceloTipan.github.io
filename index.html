About

I'm a software developer based in Quito, Ecuador, working fully remote. Day to day I build and maintain backend services for a logistics company in Mexico, mostly in C# and .NET with RabbitMQ and PostgreSQL, and Python services with FastAPI.

I studied Computer Graphics Engineering at Universidad Central del Ecuador. My thesis and a 2022 paper in Revista Odigos were about detecting body movement with computer vision to control a virtual reality application. That idea keeps coming back in my side projects: the game on this page can be played by moving a paddle in front of your webcam.
